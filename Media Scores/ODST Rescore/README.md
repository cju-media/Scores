## Halo 3: ODST Opening Rescore

[Watch The Cue Here](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yPkY2KzNnx8)

Halo 3: ODST has a special place in my heart. I adored the game as a preteen and I will always look back on my first experience fondly. The music of the night missions, with the noir sax lines soaring over light accompaniment to the background of rain covering the sky. I was in love even as a child.

I wanted to work on my film scoring skills and this scene stood out to me as a good exercise. Three distinct sections and a good bit of telegraphed action meant a dynamic scene with a lot of options to hit. One thing missing from the Halo 3: ODST score as a whole was the Halo Trilogy theme. I understand from a distinction standpoint why you would avoid using the theme in this action-packed opening. However in this small cue I am attacking I do not have to think about the broader implications of using some motivic elements. So I worked in a variation of the Halo theme in a couple of places that felt very "Halo." I kept the cue as a whole very much in the style of the original material, mostly orchestral instrumentation with light electronics.
